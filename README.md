NYC Taxi Revenue, Mobility, Demand Surge, and Congestion Analysis

Goal: This project aims to deliver a data driven analysis of NYC taxi revenue and mobility that include the highest paying routes, borough fleet imbalance, passenger credit card tip chance by time of day, etc. By finding these metrics, we can presenting actionable information, giving NYC taxi drivers a better understanding of where passengers want to travel to from pick up to drop off, why certain routes are profitable while others are not, the time of day in which drivers are most needed, and the yield difference between intra-city routes and airport routes. 

Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/diishasiing/revenue-for-cab-drivers
